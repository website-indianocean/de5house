Security

Responsible AI / human control

Automation should increase control, not remove it.

Execution authority should match risk. The more consequential an action is, the more deliberate the controls should become.

LOW RISK

Automatic

AI or automation may execute when the action is routine, reversible and well-bounded.

  • Routine information
  • Data synchronization
  • Report generation
MEDIUM RISK

Logged / reviewed

Execution can proceed with stronger logging, constraints or review depending on the workflow.

  • Customer recommendation
  • Workflow changes
  • Outbound drafts
HIGH RISK

Human approval

Important or sensitive actions should require explicit human authorization.

  • Important financial action
  • Contract commitment
  • Sensitive customer issue
  • Major operational decision
